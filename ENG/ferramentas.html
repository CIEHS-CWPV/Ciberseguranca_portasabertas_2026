Section 1 – Operational Systems


Operating systems tailored for cybersecurity are specialized environments that offer advanced features for penetration testing, privacy, and data protection. They are fundamental for professionals in the field, as they allow them to simulate attacks, identify vulnerabilities, and implement defense measures within controlled environments.

Kali Linux

A Linux distribution geared toward penetration testing and ethical hacking. It includes tools such as Nmap, Metasploit, and Wireshark.

Parrot OS

An operating system focused on privacy and security, featuring specific editions for pentesting and cloud use.

Qubes OS

A security-oriented OS that isolates virtual environments, ensuring greater protection against attacks and minimizing risks.

Windows Server

Geared toward corporate environments, it offers advanced security and network management features.

Section 2 – Softwares and Tools


In addition to operating systems, there are software applications and tools that play an essential role in digital protection. They help detect threats, monitor networks, and safeguard sensitive information, being utilized by everyday users, corporations, and governments alike.

Antivirus

Programs like Kaspersky and Bitdefender detect and remove malicious software, protecting devices against viruses and Trojans.

Firewalls

Solutions like pfSense and Cisco ASA act as barriers that filter network traffic and block unauthorized access.

Wireshark

A network traffic analysis tool that allows professionals to monitor packets and identify vulnerabilities.

Metasploit

A framework used for penetration testing, allowing professionals to simulate attacks and assess system security.
