Cibersecurity

We live in an era where practically all of our activities are connected to the internet: studying, working, shopping, communicating, and even managing our healthcare. While this reliance on technology brings countless benefits, it also exposes individuals and organizations to increasingly greater risks. It is within this landscape that cybersecurity becomes fundamental, ensuring that data and systems remain protected against cyberattacks.

Current Landscape


In recent years, cyberattacks have grown in scale and sophistication. Scams such as phishing, ransomware, and corporate data breaches cause billions of dollars in losses, affecting everyone from small businesses to governments. Digital security is no longer just a technical issue; it has become a strategic priority capable of influencing the global economy and public trust in digital services.

Key Aspects of Cybersecurity

  • ● Personal Data Protection: Preventing the theft of sensitive information such as passwords and government IDs.
  • ● Corporate Security: Preventing corporate secrets and customer data from being compromised.
  • ● National Defense: Protecting critical infrastructure, such as energy, transportation, and healthcare, against coordinated attacks.
  • ● Digital Trust: Allowing users to navigate and use online services with peace of mind.
